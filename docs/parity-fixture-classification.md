## How to read the verdicts

- **Keep** means the pinned behaviour is byte-for-byte the same in 2.1 and the group stays a parity gate unchanged.
- **Retire** means the pinned behaviour is a rule 2.1 replaced with a different one; the group's golden no longer describes 2.1. Retiring is not deleting — it records that the group must not be replayed against a 2.1 port as-is.
- **Split** means the group pins several behaviours and only some of them survived to 2.1. The kept steps form a valid 2.1 gate; the retired steps must be dropped (or moved to a 2.1-specific fixture) for the group to remain a gate.
- **Needs human decision** means the 2.1 doc does not settle a rule the group exercises, or does not settle the fate of an aggregate fixture — the verdict cannot be derived from `docs/beermat-2.1-re.md` alone.

## Notes on scope

- The wealth-cap mugging in 04 and the gun-dealer removal in 05/12 are 2.1 removals recorded in the "Arrival events" section of `docs/beermat-2.1-re.md`, not N-id rows; they are why those groups retire.
- 08 and 09 are "needs human decision" because of state-layout and aggregate-gate scope. 02 is "needs human decision" because the 2.1 doc does not re-read buying and selling. Row 11 carries the same label for its deposit and withdraw steps.

## Cross-cutting: full-state deep equality (needs human decision)

Every step's `expect.state` is a deep-equal against the full state snapshot (`tests/fixtures/README.md`, "File format"), and step 0 of every group is a `newGame` whose snapshot carries `guns` and a single `debt`. 2.1 replaces `guns` with a weapon set (N-12), replaces the single debt with loan records (N-06), and adds kidneys, an infection counter and shark release state. A step can therefore pin an unchanged rule and still fail when the state shape moves. Whether the kept steps are re-recorded against the 2.1 state, compared on a projection of the fields they exercise, or run until the shape changes is a decision for TASK-013.02.01 and applies to every group above, including those marked keep-side. No group is marked plain **keep** for this reason.
